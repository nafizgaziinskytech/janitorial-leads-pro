FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about lead quality, coverage, pricing, and how fast you'll start seeing appointments.

Are there any long-term contracts or setup fees?

No. There are no long-term contracts or setup fees. Choose the appointment package that fits your capacity, and pay only for appointments you visit and quote.

What makes your janitorial leads different from leads I could buy elsewhere?

We provide scheduled commercial cleaning appointments, not raw contact lists. Each opportunity is matched to your service area and booked with a decision-maker who is actively considering a cleaning provider.

How do you verify a lead before it's sent to me?

We confirm the contact details, business address, facility location, cleaning need, hiring intent, and appointment time before delivery.

Are these exclusive leads, or do you sell the same lead to my competitors?

Every appointment is 100% exclusive to your cleaning business. We never resell or share the same lead with competing contractors.

How fast can I start receiving cleaning leads after I sign up?

Once your target area and campaign details are confirmed, your plan is activated. Verified appointments are then delivered in real time as they are booked.

Do you generate leads in my specific city or service area?

Yes. You can target the cities, ZIP codes, counties, or service areas where your team wants to win more commercial cleaning contracts.
